Our Story

A Prescott institution since 1998.

The Riverside Kitchen started as a six-table lunch counter, and twenty-eight years later it's still run by the same family — just with more tables and a bigger oven.

We buy eggs from a farm ten minutes up the road, bake our own bread and pies every morning, and still write the daily specials on the same chalkboard we've had since day one.

  • Everything made from scratch, every morning
  • Local eggs, local produce when it's in season
  • Kid-friendly, wheelchair accessible, free parking out back
