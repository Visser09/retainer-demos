About

Part of Spencerville for over 30 years.

The Pantry's own site puts it simply: "For more than 30 years, The Village Pantry has been an essential part of life in Spencerville." It is a general store, a bakery and a deli under one roof.

People come for the cinnamon buns and the fresh pizza, and stay customers because a village store that stocks the essentials saves a trip out of town.
