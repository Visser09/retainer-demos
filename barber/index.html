The Shop

An old-school shop on King Street.

King & Main opened in 2017 with four chairs, a secondhand cash register, and one rule: never rush a cut. Eight years later the chairs are busier but the rule hasn't changed.

It's the kind of place where the coffee's always on, the sports are always on the TV, and half our regulars just stop in to talk. First-timers leave as regulars — that's the whole business plan.
