About

Made by Sue Torrance, in Prescott.

Tasty Indulgences has no storefront. Sue Torrance runs it from a commercial kitchen in her home in Prescott, and her desserts and comfort-food meals are sold through local stores and restaurants instead.

Her Google listing describes the range plainly: premium fresh and frozen, convenient gluten-free meals, soups and desserts. Customers rate it 5.0 stars across 25 reviews, and praise the chicken pot pie, tourtiere, cinnamon buns and gluten-free desserts.

  • All meals, soups and desserts are gluten free
  • Sold at New Horizons, Little Sisterz and The Community Market
  • Orders and questions by phone or email
